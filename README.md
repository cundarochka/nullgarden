# Nullgarden

> **Nothing disappears quietly.**

A quieter approach to keeping things where they belong.

Nullgarden is a small, personal-use utility project built around a simple idea: important things deserve a little more continuity and a little less uncertainty.

Designed to stay out of your way, work quietly in the background, and keep things simple.

### Explore

- [Project homepage](./index.html)
- [Privacy statement](./privacy.html)

### Principles

- **Quiet by design** — less routine, fewer things to remember.
- **Your space, your rules** — built around infrastructure you already use.
- **Small by design** — focused functionality without unnecessary complexity.
- **Privacy matters** — no analytics or advertising tracking is intentionally built into the application.

### Privacy

Nullgarden is intended for personal use and does not collect or share data for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

When Google Drive integration is used, files selected for backup are transferred to the Google Drive account and destination authorized by the user. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to that service.

Read the full [Privacy Statement](./privacy.html).
