The short version

Nullgarden is intended for personal use. The application is designed to operate without collecting analytics or tracking user activity, and it does not sell or share personal information for advertising.

When you use Google Drive integration, the files you choose to back up are transferred to the Google Drive account and destination you authorize. This transfer is part of the application's intended functionality and is subject to Google's own terms and privacy policy.

This project does not operate a hosted account service or intentionally collect personal profiles. You can inspect the source code to understand how the application handles data.
